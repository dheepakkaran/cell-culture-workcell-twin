## Honest notes / limits
- Execution is kinematic (path playback + kinematic weld); physics is used only for release/settle.
- Cycle time is simulated (1.0 rad/s joint cap + fixed gripper/settle times), not hardware time.
- 100/100 success reflects mild randomization; missing caps are the only injected fault that the
  pipeline must catch. Placement error is near zero by construction (grasp offset measured at grasp).
- Grasp yaw is the nominal slot yaw (flask yaw noise ±5° is not estimated by vision).
- No ROS 2, MoveIt, pipettes, decapping, confluency model, or real hardware (out of scope).
